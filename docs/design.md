# PS5 interactive study

The requested result is a local interactive 3D viewer that follows the four supplied product photographs. Images 1 and 2 show the Slim disc and Original disc silhouettes; images 3 and 4 show the Pro in vertical and horizontal positions. Default to the Pro and make all three designs selectable instead of mixing details from different generations.

Visual thesis: an off-white photographic studio with graphite typography, cobalt accents, and a large softly lit physical model.

Content plan: one full-screen workspace; a quiet console selector and inspector beside the model; orientation and inspection tools under the model; a reference drawer containing the original photographs.

Interaction thesis: damped orbit and zoom, animated orientation changes, and an optional slow turntable. Respect reduced motion. Provide camera reset, controller visibility, two studio backgrounds, and an image export.

Geometry: custom curved and thick side plates surrounding a separate rounded black chassis. Original uses continuous flared plates and an integrated disc housing; Slim uses two white sections and its smaller body; Pro uses three recessed cooling ribs. Include front ports and buttons, rear ventilation and connection details, subtle blue edge lights, a base, a PlayStation emblem, and a shaped DualSense controller with touchpad, analog sticks, D-pad, symbol buttons, triggers, and speaker holes.

The mesh is a visual reconstruction from the supplied photographs, not an official engineering model. Source and images stay in this project; no hosting is requested.
